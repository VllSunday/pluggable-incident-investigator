# Dashboard Direction Contract

## THESIS

Incident investigation should feel like unfolding one auditable case file, not scanning an interchangeable SaaS dashboard. The visual hierarchy makes evidence and human decision points more prominent than chrome.

## OWN-WORLD

**The Unfolding Case File.** Warm diagnostic paper, sumi-like ink, vermilion review marks, fold-gold creases, visible construction lines, numbered steps, and square instrument controls form the durable world. The metaphor is structural: every conclusion can be traced backward through the preceding folds.

## STORY

A source event enters as one compact signal. The page unfolds through evidence, competing hypotheses, reflection, safe action, and outcome. The active step breathes; completed and idle steps remain still. The operator can inspect the record and approve or reject impact without leaving the path.

## FIRST VIEWPORT

On desktop, the first viewport shows the incident queue at left, the selected incident and the beginning of its numbered investigation spine in the dominant center, and technical identifiers at right. On mobile, the same order becomes queue → selected case → detail, with a compact RU/EN control retained in the top bar.

## FORM

Concept seed key: `350841d9`. The assigned fold-sequence direction is implemented as clipped paper stages connected by a numbered crease. A restrained construction grid makes the surface read as a diagnostic workbench; the clipped corner, product mark, and state nodes reuse the same geometry. Controls stay semantic and keyboard-operable, and all motion stops under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
